Wednesday, December 11, 2024

Sad News: The Last Dervish of Qadian

Session 21 of 22 2 min read Postscript

Today, a solemn news reached us: Respected Tayyib Ali Sahib, the last Dervish of Qadian, has passed away by the decree of Allah.

Indeed, to Allah we belong and to Him shall we return.

May Allah grant him forgiveness, elevate his ranks, and bestow upon him a high place in Paradise. Ameen, Thumma Ameen. May Allah accept the sacrifices of all the Darweshan and grant the next generation the ability to uphold their noble traditions.

The Caller is the Most Beloved; O heart, dedicate yourself to Him.

A Historical Context

In 1947, during the Partition of India, Muslims were moving to Pakistan, while Hindus and Sikhs relocated to India. Qadian, the birthplace of the Ahmadiyya Muslim Community, remained in Indian Punjab.

Recognizing the significance of Qadian as the spiritual center of Ahmadiyyat, Hazrat Musleh Maud (ra), the Second Khalifa of the Promised Messiah (as), made a historic decision. He instructed 313 devoted individuals, later known as Darweshan-e-Qadian, to stay behind in Qadian. These Darweshan were tasked with safeguarding the sacred sites, protecting Jamaat properties, and preserving the spiritual heritage of Qadian during a time of great turmoil and uncertainty.

Their sacrifices were immense. They lived simple, selfless lives, enduring hardships and challenges with unwavering faith. Their dedication ensured that the birthplace of the Promised Messiah (as) remained a vibrant center of spirituality and history.

Meeting the Last Dervish

During our recent trip to Qadian (November 21—December 1, 2024), we had the honor of meeting Tayyib Ali Sahib. He was seated in a wheelchair near Darul Masih. Though frail with age, his presence was profoundly moving. He graciously allowed us to embrace him, shake his hand, and even take photographs with him.

It is humbling to think that he was a living connection to the sacrifices made during the turbulent times of partition. His unwavering commitment to the mission of the Promised Messiah (as) ensured that the legacy of Ahmadiyyat remained intact.

Reflections on the Darweshan

Most of the people we met during our time in Qadian—the shopkeepers, students, and Jamaat workers—are the children and grandchildren of these noble Darweshan. Their lives continue to embody the sacrifices and spirit of devotion passed down through generations.

As we reflect on Tayyib Ali Sahib’s passing, we are reminded of the enormity of the responsibility these individuals carried. They were not just custodians of property; they were guardians of history, culture, and the mission of the Promised Messiah (as).

With Tayyib Ali Sahib’s passing, an era has come to an end. Yet, the legacy of the Darweshan lives on in the hearts of Ahmadis worldwide.

Long live Qadian.

Long live the legacy of Darweshan-e-Qadian.
